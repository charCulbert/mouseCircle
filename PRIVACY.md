# Mouse Circle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Mouse Circle does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access.
- Your settings (circle size, colour, animation and shortcut choices) are stored only on your Mac, in the app's own preferences, and never leave it.
- The app listens for mouse movement and clicks solely to draw the circle on screen. It does not record, log, or send this information anywhere.
- The app does not read keystrokes. The optional keyboard shortcut is registered through the system hot key service, which only tells the app when that specific key combination is pressed.

If you have any questions, open an issue at https://github.com/charCulbert/mouseCircle/issues or email charlieculbertmusic@gmail.com.
